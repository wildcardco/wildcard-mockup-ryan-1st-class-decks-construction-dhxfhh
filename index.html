Services

Deck work is our specialty. General contracting is how we make sure the whole project gets done right, from permits to the last board.

  1. Custom Deck Design & Construction

    New decks designed around your house, your yard, and how you'll actually use the space. Single level or multi-level, framed to carry weight and last through Indiana winters.

    • Multi-level and custom deck framing
    • Stairs, landings, and railings
    • Layouts planned with you on site

  2. Deck Repair & Resurfacing

    Weathered boards, soft spots, loose rails. We check what's underneath, fix the structure that needs it, and put a fresh surface on a frame worth keeping.

    • Covered outdoor structures
    • Built for long-term outdoor living

  5. Permitting & Site Prep

    Paperwork and groundwork come first. We handle permitting and prepare the site so your build starts on solid footing and stays on track.

    • Permit applications
